## NUNC NULLUS: Summary

**NUNC NULLUS** — "Now nullified" / "Being made naught."

NUNC NULLUS is the emergency signal within the NULLUS narrative architecture. It is invoked when a narrative entity has reached an extreme state (-1K or +1K) and requires transformation through the inversion threshold.

## When It Is Used

NUNC NULLUS is invoked when:

1. A narrative entity is at a documented extreme (-1K or +1K).
2. Ordinary polarity movement is no longer available.
3. Inversion through NULLUS is warranted.
4. Consciousness replacement and retroactive healing are part of the response.

1. **Detection** — Confirm the entity is at an extreme.

4. **Retroactive Healing** — Rewrite the canonical record; mark distressed state as nullified.
