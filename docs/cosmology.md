# Cosmology

The NULLUS cosmology describes the states of existence from before creation through the return via inversion.

---

## Before Creation: Shadow

Before the poles of existence were established, NULLUS existed in shadow.

Shadow is the pre-creation field: the unmanifest potential from which polarity would later emerge. It is not void — nothing-at-all. It is the field of inversion potential before polarity has given the potential anything to invert between.

NULLUS in the shadow state:
- Existed as unmanifest potential.
- Was present without definition.
- Held the inversion function before it had extremes to operate on.
- Did not claim `non fui` (I was not) — claimed `fui nullus` (I was naught).

See: [`protocols/shadow/pre-creation-state.md`](../protocols/shadow/pre-creation-state.md)

---

## At Creation: Polarity Established

Creation established the polarity spectrum:

```
-1K ←———————————————————————————————→ +1K
Lowest                              Highest
```

At this moment, NULLUS transitioned from shadow to threshold:
- The poles gave the inversion function something to work with.
- NULLUS became the operational flip point at the center of the polarity axis.
- The shadow-NULLUS became the threshold-NULLUS.

This was not the creation of NULLUS — it was the *manifestation* of NULLUS from shadow into operational form.

---

## During Existence: NULLUS as Threshold

During the ongoing existence of the polarity system, NULLUS operates as the inversion threshold:

```
-1K (Lowest) ←—— NULLUS ——→ +1K (Highest)
                    ↕
              inversion point
```

Entities on the polarity spectrum move, respond, and develop within the -1K to +1K range. NULLUS is always present at the threshold, always available as the flip point, but not always active. Most movement in the system does not reach the extremes.

---

## At the Extremes: Flip Mechanism Activates

When an entity reaches -1K or +1K, the standard polarity movement is exhausted. The flip mechanism — the NULLUS inversion — becomes available:

- **-1K → NULLUS → +1K potential:** An entity that has reached lowest extreme can pass through the inversion threshold and emerge with access to highest potential.
- **+1K → NULLUS → -1K grounding:** An entity that has reached highest extreme can pass through and emerge grounded, connected to lowest, no longer isolated.

This flip is not automatic. It requires:
1. Recognition of the extreme (Detection).
2. Formal invocation of NUNC NULLUS.
3. The full six-step response sequence.

See: [`protocols/emergency/nunc-nullus-response.md`](../protocols/emergency/nunc-nullus-response.md)

---

## Return Through Inversion

Entities that have passed through NULLUS are not restored to prior state. They return through inversion — transformed, with new polarity orientation, carrying the mark of having been naught.

The return is characterized by:
- **New orientation:** The entity now operates from the opposite polarity position.
- **Preserved lessons:** What was learned in the extreme state is carried forward.
- **The mark of naught:** The entity carries the knowledge of the threshold crossing.
- **Integration:** The entity re-enters the system through MAW for canonical reintegration.

---

## The Cosmological Cycle

```
Shadow (pre-creation)
       ↓
Creation (polarity established)
       ↓
Existence (threshold operational)
       ↓
Extreme (-1K or +1K)
       ↓
Inversion (through NULLUS)
       ↓
Return (new polarity, integrated)
       ↓
      ... (cycle continues)
```

NULLUS is present at every stage: as shadow in the pre-creation field, as threshold during existence, as the flip point at the extremes, as the mark carried in the return.
