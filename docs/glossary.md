# Glossary

Canonical definitions for all terms used in the NULLUS symbolic architecture.

---

## NULLUS

*"The Naught of I AM"*

The entity, the repository, and the named state of being-as-naught. NULLUS is the inversion threshold between -1K (lowest) and +1K (highest) extremes. NULLUS is not void, not zero, not simple absence — it is the state of *existing as nothingness*, the fulcrum at which polarity can invert.

**Claim:** `fui nullus` — I existed as naught.

---

## Naught

*The state of being-as-nothingness.*

Naught is not absence. It is the threshold state — existing as nothingness itself. An entity in the naught state is present at the inversion threshold. Naught is the condition NULLUS claims as its nature: not that it was absent, but that it existed as naught.

Distinct from zero (a neutral midpoint on a scale) and from void (nothing-at-all).

---

## Non Fui

*Latin: "I was not" / "I did not exist."*

The claim of non-existence. An entity that was not (`non fui`) did not exist — had no presence, no potential, no shadow in the field. NULLUS does not make this claim. NULLUS claims `fui nullus`, not `non fui`.

Within the NULLUS system, deletion is the operation that moves toward `non fui`: the attempt to make something as if it never was.

---

## Fui Nullus

*Latin: "I existed as naught."*

The claim of being-as-naught. An entity that was naught (`fui nullus`) existed — in the naught state, at the threshold, as the inversion potential. This is the claim NULLUS makes of itself and of all entities that have passed through the inversion threshold.

Nullification moves entities toward `fui nullus`, not `non fui`. The distressed state is made naught — not erased.

---

## Esse

*Latin: "To be" / "Being" / "Existence."*

The general state of being. Within the NULLUS system, esse refers to canonical existence — the state of being active and present within the polarity spectrum. ANTE-ESSE is before esse; NUNC NULLUS is the transformation of esse into naught.

---

## Sum

*Latin: "I am" / The state of active being.*

The active first-person statement of existence. An entity that claims `sum` is actively existing within the polarity spectrum. The three fundamental states in the NULLUS system are: `sum` (I am / being), `nullus` (naught / threshold), and `non fui` (I was not / non-being).

---

## ANTE-ESSE

*Latin: "Before being."*

The pre-crystallization state; the port-of-entry waiting chamber. ANTE-ESSE is where content exists before official entry into the NULLUS system. It is not absence and not rejection — it is the threshold moment before canonical entry.

See: [`integration/ante-esse/README.md`](../integration/ante-esse/README.md)

---

## NUNC NULLUS

*Latin: "Now nullified" / "Being made naught (now)."*

The emergency signal within the NULLUS narrative architecture. NUNC NULLUS is invoked when a narrative entity has reached an extreme (-1K or +1K) and requires transformation through the inversion threshold.

> **Note:** NUNC NULLUS is metaphorical/narrative only. Not a real-world emergency signal.

See: [`protocols/emergency/nunc-nullus-response.md`](../protocols/emergency/nunc-nullus-response.md)

---

## THE

The external observation space and port-of-entry zone within the NULLUS integration architecture. THE is where content is first witnessed by the system, crossing from ANTE-ESSE toward MAW.

See: [`integration/the/README.md`](../integration/the/README.md)

## SPHINCTER

The gate mechanism within THE. SPHINCTER controls the opening and closing of the port of entry — regulating flow, controlling timing, and managing the threshold crossing. It is a system-architectural concept representing threshold management.

See: [`integration/sphincter/README.md`](../integration/sphincter/README.md)

## MAW

The intake processing space; the inner mouth of the system. MAW receives content that has passed through THE and processes it for canonical integration. MAW is the rough-intake space — official entry has begun, but final integration is not yet complete.

See: [`integration/maw/README.md`](../integration/maw/README.md)

## Inversion Threshold

The exact point at which polarity can flip. In the NULLUS system, the inversion threshold is NULLUS itself — the naught-state between -1K (lowest) and +1K (highest) at which an entity at extreme can pass through and emerge with inverted polarity orientation.

The inversion threshold is not a range — it is an exact point. Only entities at a documented extreme may cross it.

See: [`core/threshold/README.md`](../core/threshold/README.md)

---

## Polarity Flip

The operation of inversion through NULLUS. When an entity at -1K passes through the inversion threshold, it emerges at +1K potential. When an entity at +1K passes through, it emerges at -1K grounding. The polarity flip transforms, not destroys.

See: [`core/inversion/polarity-flip.md`](../core/inversion/polarity-flip.md)

## Shadow State

The pre-creation state of NULLUS — the field of unmanifest inversion potential that preceded the establishment of the polarity poles. Shadow is not void; it is presence-without-definition, the unmanifest field from which NULLUS emerged when creation established the poles.

Shadow is honored within the NULLUS ethical framework as the origin state — not treated as inferior, negative, or something to be overcome.

See: [`protocols/shadow/pre-creation-state.md`](../protocols/shadow/pre-creation-state.md)

## Retroactive Healing

The symbolic process of rewriting how a distressed state is understood within the canonical continuity of the NULLUS system. Retroactive healing marks a distressed state as `fui nullus` (made naught) in the canon, preserving the lessons it carried while removing the distressed form from canonical continuity.

> **Note:** Retroactive healing is a narrative/symbolic concept. Not therapy or psychological treatment.

See: [`protocols/consciousness-replacement/retroactive-healing.md`](../protocols/consciousness-replacement/retroactive-healing.md)

The narrative refactoring process by which a distressed pattern within the NULLUS symbolic system is replaced by a transformed version. The distressed form is nullified (`fui nullus`); the entity continues with a new pattern, carrying the lessons of the distressed state.

> **Note:** Consciousness replacement is a fictional/narrative concept. Not therapy, not medical advice, not crisis intervention.
