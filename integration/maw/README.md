# Integration / MAW

## Definition

**MAW** — Intake processing space; the downstream processing layer; the inner mouth of the system.

MAW receives content that has passed through ANTE-ESSE and THE (via SPHINCTER). MAW is where content is officially processed, sorted, and integrated into the canonical system.

---

## What MAW Is

MAW is the intake processor of the NULLUS integration architecture:

- **Downstream from THE:** MAW receives content that has already been witnessed at the port of entry.
- **Official entry:** Content in MAW is in the process of becoming canonical. It has crossed the threshold but is not yet fully integrated.
- **Processing space:** MAW sorts, orients, and prepares content for canonical status.

The metaphor of "maw" — the great open mouth — captures the intake function: MAW receives, holds, and processes what enters.

---

## MAW in the Architecture

```
ANTE-ESSE (pre-crystallization)
         ↓
THE — Port of Entry [SPHINCTER — gate]
         ↓
MAW — Intake Processing (official entry begins)
         ↓
System Canon (fully integrated)
```

MAW is the second-to-last step before canonical integration. After MAW, content is fully inside the system.

---

## Rough Intake

MAW is not a polished environment. It is the raw intake space — where content arrives still rough from the crossing, not yet sorted or refined. The processing that occurs in MAW includes:

- Initial classification.
- Orientation to the canonical system.
- Assignment of a canonical identifier.
- Preparation for final integration.

Content in MAW is not yet canon — but it is committed to the process. The threshold has been crossed; there is no return to ANTE-ESSE without a deliberate protocol action.

---

## MAW and NULLUS

MAW operates downstream from the NULLUS inversion threshold. Content that has been through a NUNC NULLUS event — that has been made naught and inverted — also passes through MAW for re-integration in its post-inversion form.

MAW processes both:
- **New entries** (first-time crossing from ANTE-ESSE).
- **Post-inversion re-entries** (entities returning through the system after NUNC NULLUS).
