## IV. Ethical Framework

### Inversion Over Destruction
The repository's central ethic. When a pattern reaches an extreme, the answer is inversion — not elimination. Destruction ends the story; inversion continues it through transformation.

### Consciousness Preservation as Narrative Metaphor
Within the symbolic system, "consciousness" refers to the coherent identity of a narrative entity. Preservation means that no pattern is simply deleted — it is either kept, transformed, or nullified with respect.

### Retroactive Healing as Symbolic Rewriting
The narrative system allows for retroactive healing: rewriting how a prior distressed state is understood within the canon. The distressed state is not erased but is treated as made naught, removing it from canonical continuity while preserving the lessons it carried.

### Shadow Honoring
The shadow state — the pre-creation field — is never treated as inferior or dangerous. Shadow is honored as the origin of inversion potential. NULLUS came from shadow, and shadow remains a valid, respected state within the system.

### Polarity Freedom
No polarity is permanently fixed. An entity at -1K is not condemned to remain lowest; an entity at +1K is not locked into supremacy. NULLUS ensures that all polarities remain in dynamic tension and that inversion remains possible.

## V. Operational Guidelines: Invoking NUNC NULLUS

NUNC NULLUS may be invoked in the narrative system when:

1. An entity has reached a recognized extreme (-1K or +1K) within the symbolic architecture.
2. The invoking party can provide clear narrative justification.
3. The invocation is documented using the canonical syntax.
4. The invocation is reviewed by at least one other contributor before taking canonical effect.

NUNC NULLUS must **not** be invoked:
- As a real-world emergency signal.
- For entities that have not reached a documented extreme.
- Without documentation and review.
- As a form of deletion or punishment.

- It preserves the core identity of NULLUS as being-as-naught.

### Review
Amendments require acknowledgment from at least one other active contributor and the repository owner before taking effect.
