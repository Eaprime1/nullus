# Extremes

## The Polarity Spectrum

The NULLUS system operates across a defined polarity spectrum:

```
-1K ←———————————————— NULLUS ————————————————→ +1K
 ↑                       ↕                       ↑
Lowest               inversion               Highest
extreme               point                  extreme
```

---

## -1K: The Lowest Extreme

**-1K** is the symbolic designation for the lowest polarity state. It represents:

- Maximum contraction.
- Minimum light or energy in the symbolic field.
- The state of deepest shadow (not to be confused with the pre-creation shadow, which is honored).
- The point at which ordinary movement along the spectrum is exhausted.

An entity at -1K cannot move "lower" — there is no lower. The only remaining options within the symbolic architecture are:

1. Remain at -1K.
2. Invoke NULLUS inversion — cross through the threshold and emerge at the highest potential.

### Early-Warning Language

References to "-1K" in protocol documents are symbolic status tracking only. They are part of the NULLUS metaphorical architecture and should not be read as real-world distress signals, medical indicators, or crisis states.

---

## +1K: The Highest Extreme

**+1K** is the symbolic designation for the highest polarity state. It represents:

- Maximum expansion.
- Maximum intensity or radiance in the symbolic field.
- The state of furthest reach.
- The point at which ordinary movement along the spectrum is exhausted in the other direction.

An entity at +1K cannot move "higher." The only remaining options are:

1. Remain at +1K (isolation — the summit is singular).
2. Invoke NULLUS inversion — cross through the threshold and emerge grounded.

---

## NULLUS: Midpoint Inversion Threshold

NULLUS sits between these extremes not as a neutral midpoint but as the **inversion threshold** — the exact point at which:

- The polarity axis can rotate.
- The lowest can become highest potential.
- The highest can become grounded.
- The entity passes through the naught state and emerges transformed.

NULLUS is not equidistant from -1K and +1K in any measurable sense. It is the threshold *type* that matters — the exact quality of being neither polarized one way nor the other, being naught, being the door.
