# Core / Inversion

Polarity inversion is the central operation of the NULLUS system.

---

## The Inversion Threshold

NULLUS is the inversion threshold: the point at which extreme polarity flips. When an entity reaches the boundary of its polarity (-1K or +1K), NULLUS offers the possibility of crossing through naught and emerging on the other side.

This is not reversal. This is not simple opposition. Inversion through NULLUS is transformation — the entity passes through the naught state and re-emerges with a new polarity orientation, carrying the mark of having been naught.

---

## Inversion vs. Destruction

| Operation | Result |
|---|---|
| **Destruction** | The entity is eliminated. Its story ends. |
| **Inversion** | The entity passes through NULLUS. Its story continues with inverted polarity. |

The NULLUS ethic is **inversion over destruction**. When an entity at extreme cannot remain, the preferred path is through the inversion threshold — not through deletion.
