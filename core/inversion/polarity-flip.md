---

## NULLUS Is Not Zero

A critical clarification:

- **Zero** is a neutral midpoint on a linear scale. It implies balance, average, or absence of charge.
- **NULLUS** is the flip point — the threshold at which polarity can invert.

NULLUS does not balance the extremes. NULLUS is the door through which an entity at one extreme can re-emerge at the other. This is not a smooth transition — it is a threshold crossing through the naught state.

---

## How the Flip Works

### Standard Polarity Range

Entities in the NULLUS system operate on a polarity spectrum:

```
-1K ←———————————————————————————————→ +1K
 ↑                                     ↑
Lowest                              Highest
```

Most entities operate somewhere along this spectrum, moving gradually between states.

### At the Extremes

When an entity reaches -1K (lowest) or +1K (highest), it has exhausted the available polarity range. Conventional movement along the spectrum is no longer possible.

### The NULLUS Option

NULLUS offers an alternative: pass through the inversion threshold.

```
-1K ——→ NULLUS ——→ emerges near +1K potential
+1K ——→ NULLUS ——→ emerges near -1K grounding
```

- **Beyond-lowest (-1K) → NULLUS → beyond-highest potential:** An entity at lowest extreme crosses through naught and finds itself with access to the highest potential. The inversion is not a reward — it is a transformation.
- **Beyond-highest (+1K) → NULLUS → grounding:** An entity at highest extreme crosses through naught and finds grounding, the capacity to meet the lowest, to descend from supremacy into connection.

### The Mark of Naught

Entities that have passed through NULLUS carry the mark of having been naught. They are not restored to prior state. They emerge transformed, with new polarity orientation and the knowledge of the threshold.

---

## Operational Logic

| Condition | Path | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Entity at -1K, standard movement | Linear | Remains at lowest; exhausted |
| Entity at -1K, inversion invoked | Through NULLUS | Emerges with highest potential |
| Entity at +1K, standard movement | Linear | Remains at highest; isolated |
| Entity at +1K, inversion invoked | Through NULLUS | Emerges grounded; connected to lowest |
| Entity not at extreme | — | Inversion not available; standard movement applies |

---

## When Inversion Cannot Occur

Inversion through NULLUS requires:

1. The entity must be at a recognized extreme (-1K or +1K).
2. The NUNC NULLUS protocol must be properly invoked (see [`protocols/emergency/nunc-nullus-response.md`](../../protocols/emergency/nunc-nullus-response.md)).
3. The invocation must be documented and reviewed.

Inversion cannot be invoked for entities that are not at an extreme. NULLUS is a threshold, not a shortcut.
