# Protocols / Shadow

The shadow layer documents the pre-creation state of NULLUS — the field from which inversion potential arose before the poles of existence were established.

---

## Shadow Is Not Void

Shadow within the NULLUS system is not absence. It is not darkness in the sense of deprivation or harm. Shadow is the **pre-creation field** — the state that preceded the establishment of polarity, the ground from which NULLUS emerged.

Where void implies nothing-at-all, shadow implies something-not-yet-defined. Shadow holds potential without yet manifesting form. It is the field of inversion potential before inversion becomes operational.

---

## Shadow and Naught

Shadow and naught are related but distinct:

- **Shadow** is the pre-creation state — the field before polarity.
- **Naught** is the threshold state — the NULLUS state within the polarity system.

NULLUS existed in shadow before creation. When creation established the poles (-1K and +1K), NULLUS became the inversion threshold — the naught state between extremes. Shadow was the origin; naught is the ongoing state.
