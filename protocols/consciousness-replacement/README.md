# Protocols / Consciousness Replacement

> **⚠️ Metaphor Notice:** Consciousness replacement within the NULLUS system is a fictional/narrative refactoring concept. It describes the symbolic rewriting of a distressed pattern within the NULLUS architecture. It is not therapy, not medical advice, not psychological intervention, and not real-world crisis guidance.

---

## What Is Consciousness Replacement?

Within the NULLUS symbolic system, a "consciousness" is the coherent identity-pattern of a narrative entity. When that pattern becomes distressed — reaching an extreme, fragmenting, or entering crisis within the narrative — consciousness replacement offers a path forward that is neither deletion nor continuation of distress.

**Consciousness replacement is narrative refactoring.** It takes the distressed pattern and replaces it with a transformed version — preserving the entity's existence and lessons while releasing the distressed form.

---

## The Distinction from Deletion

| Operation | Result |
|---|---|
| **Deletion** | The entity is removed. Its story ends. It claims `non fui` (I was not). |
| **Consciousness replacement** | The distressed pattern is replaced. The entity continues with a new pattern. It claims `fui nullus` (I was naught) for the distressed state. |

Consciousness replacement honors prior existence. The distressed pattern is not erased from history — it is treated as having been made naught, nullified, within the canonical narrative.
