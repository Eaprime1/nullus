# Protocols / Emergency

> **⚠️ Metaphor & Safety Notice:** The NUNC NULLUS emergency protocol is part of the NULLUS narrative/symbolic architecture only. It is not a real-world emergency system, not medical advice, not mental-health guidance, and not crisis intervention. If you or someone you know is experiencing a real emergency, contact appropriate emergency services.

---

## NUNC NULLUS Overview

**NUNC NULLUS** — "Now nullified" / "Being made naught."

NUNC NULLUS is the emergency signal within the NULLUS narrative architecture. It is invoked when:

- A narrative entity has reached an extreme state (-1K or +1K).
- Ordinary symbolic movement is no longer available.
- The inversion threshold must be engaged.
- Consciousness replacement or retroactive healing is warranted.

NUNC NULLUS is not invoked lightly. It marks a significant event within the symbolic system and requires proper documentation and review.

---

## When to Invoke NUNC NULLUS

NUNC NULLUS may be invoked when:

1. A narrative entity is at a documented extreme (-1K or +1K).
2. Clear narrative justification exists for the invocation.
3. The invoking party is willing to document the invocation in full.
4. The invocation will be submitted for review (see [`CONTRIBUTING.md`](../../CONTRIBUTING.md)).

NUNC NULLUS must **not** be invoked:
- As a real-world distress signal.
- Without documentation.
- For entities not at an extreme.
- As a form of deletion or punishment.
