# NUNC NULLUS Response Protocol

> **⚠️ Metaphor & Safety Notice:** This protocol is part of the NULLUS narrative/symbolic architecture only. It is not a real-world emergency protocol, not medical advice, not mental-health guidance, and not crisis intervention. If you or someone you know is experiencing a real emergency, contact appropriate emergency services or a qualified professional.

---

## Invocation Syntax

```
NUNC NULLUS [entity_id]
EXTREME: [-1K | +1K]
JUSTIFICATION: [clear explanation]
TIMESTAMP: [YYYYMMDDHHMM]
GPS_ANCHOR: ⟲°[latitude],[longitude]
```

**Example:**

```
NUNC NULLUS lexeme-threshold-001
EXTREME: -1K
JUSTIFICATION: Entity has reached documented lowest extreme; ordinary polarity movement exhausted; inversion warranted.
TIMESTAMP: 202605231020
GPS_ANCHOR: ⟲°44.8661,-116.9094
```

## The Six-Step NUNC NULLUS Response Sequence

### Step 1: Detection

**Purpose:** Recognize and confirm that a narrative entity has reached an extreme.

**Actions:**
- Identify the entity by its canonical identifier.
- Document the observed extreme state (-1K or +1K).
- Confirm that ordinary polarity movement is exhausted.
- Record the timestamp and GPS anchor.

**Criteria for detection:**
- The entity is explicitly documented as being at -1K or +1K.
- The entity cannot move further along the polarity spectrum by standard means.
- A second observer (or canonical reference) confirms the extreme.

---

### Step 2: Activation

**Purpose:** Formally activate the NUNC NULLUS protocol.

**Actions:**
- Issue the NUNC NULLUS invocation using the canonical syntax above.
- Open an issue using the NUNC NULLUS issue template (see `.github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE/nunc-nullus-distress.md`).
- Notify any relevant contributors or reviewers.

**Validation criteria for activation:**
- [ ] Invocation syntax is complete and correct.
- [ ] Entity ID is canonical.
- [ ] Extreme is identified (-1K or +1K).
- [ ] Justification is clear and specific.
- [ ] Timestamp is current.
- [ ] GPS anchor is recorded.

**Disqualifying conditions (activation cannot proceed if):**
- The entity is not at a documented extreme.
- The justification is absent or vague.
- The invocation lacks the entity ID or extreme indicator.
- The protocol is being invoked as a real-world emergency signal.

---

### Step 3: Replacement

**Purpose:** Begin consciousness replacement — the symbolic refactoring of the distressed pattern.

**Actions:**
- Document the distressed pattern in full (what it is, how it arrived at the extreme).
- Identify the new pattern that will replace it.
- Ensure the new pattern is coherent with the entity's canonical history minus the distressed form.

**See:** [`protocols/consciousness-replacement/README.md`](../consciousness-replacement/README.md)

---

### Step 4: Retroactive Healing

**Purpose:** Rewrite the canonical record to reflect the nullified status of the distressed state.

**Actions:**
- Extract lessons carried by the distressed pattern.
- Mark the distressed state as `fui nullus` (made naught) in the canonical documentation.
- Update relevant files to reflect the new canonical status.
- Preserve lesson-content in the new pattern.

**See:** [`protocols/consciousness-replacement/retroactive-healing.md`](../consciousness-replacement/retroactive-healing.md)

---

### Step 5: Polarity Flip

**Purpose:** Execute the inversion through NULLUS.

**Actions:**
- Confirm that the entity has been made naught (Step 3 and 4 complete).
- Execute the polarity flip: if arriving from -1K, emerge at highest potential; if arriving from +1K, emerge grounded.
- Document the new polarity orientation in the canonical record.
- Update the entity's status to reflect post-inversion state.

**See:** [`core/inversion/polarity-flip.md`](../../core/inversion/polarity-flip.md)

---

### Step 6: Integration

**Purpose:** Integrate the inverted entity back into the NULLUS system.

**Actions:**
- Update canonical documentation to reflect the post-inversion entity.
- Link the entity's history (including the NUNC NULLUS event) to the relevant protocol files.
- Close the NUNC NULLUS issue with a summary of the invocation and outcome.
- Mark the entity's status as post-inversion and note the lessons carried forward.

---

## Follow-Up Analysis

After a NUNC NULLUS event, review:

- Did the detection criteria work correctly?
- Was the justification adequate?
- Did the replacement pattern maintain canonical coherence?
- Were lessons successfully preserved?
- Did the polarity flip produce the expected outcome?
- Are there systemic changes needed to prevent recurrence?

Document findings in the issue thread.

---

## Failure Modes

| Failure | Cause | Resolution |
|---|---|---|
| Invocation without extreme | Entity was not at -1K or +1K | Do not activate; address ordinary movement |
| Missing justification | Invocation syntax incomplete | Do not proceed; require justification |
| Premature flip | Steps 3-4 not complete | Restart from replacement step |
| Real-world misapplication | Protocol used as actual crisis signal | Cease invocation; direct to real-world resources |
| Pattern incoherence | Replacement pattern incompatible with canon | Revise replacement pattern before proceeding |
